# Beagle Brain Template (BBT)

[Beagle Brain Template](https://zuozt.github.io/BBT-template/)

**A high-resolution MRI brain template for adult Beagle**

An MRI resource for canine neuroimaging research, including high-resolution in vivo T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and myelin templates, with associated tissue probability maps, labels, and masks.

## Overview

Canines, which exhibit similar emotional and social processing to humans, are becoming important animal models for neuroscience research. Beagles are commonly used in laboratory studies. The Beagle Brain Template (BBT) provides high-resolution structural MRI references generated from purebred Beagles to support investigations of canine brain structure and function.

## Terms of use

The original BBT webpage describes the resource as available for **academic research and noncommercial purposes only**, and **not intended for clinical diagnosis**. Uses outside these terms require written permission from the rights holder. Please retain proper citation when using the templates in scholarly work. Review the original terms and distribution permissions before publicly uploading the archives.
