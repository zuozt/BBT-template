Overview

An MRI resource intended to support canine neuroimaging research.

Canines, which exhibit similar emotional and social processing to humans, are becoming one of the preferred animal models for neuroscience research. Beagles are the most common laboratory canine, due to their moderate size, docile nature, and strong immunity. However, there is currently no MRI brain template for the purebred Beagle, which hinders their use in studies involving neuroimaging. Here, we present the Beagle Brain Template (BBT), which consists of high resolution in vivo T1-weighted and T2-weighted templates, as well as a myelin template, generated from purebred Beagles. This high-resolution purebred canine brain template lays the foundation for future studies aimed at in vivo analyses of the brain structure and function of Beagle dogs.
The original webpage describes data supplied in ZIP archives containing NIfTI (.nii) files. Template image previews and ZIP archives have not yet been supplied for this redesigned site.
